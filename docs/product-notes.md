# Leash product notes

- Since 4.12 (Mon Nov 2), owners get a Walk Done alert when the walk ends, with a photo and the minutes walked. An email receipt follows every charge.
- Since 4.12, a walk counts only if it lasts at least 20 minutes. Minutes are measured from the walker's GPS. If it doesn't count, the owner isn't charged and the walker isn't paid.
- Walks auto-cancel when a lightning alert is active (since 4.11). The owner is refunded automatically; refunds settle in 3 to 5 business days.
- Tips are on the walk summary screen. Dog profiles are under Settings > Pets.
- What shipped and when is in docs/release-notes.md.
