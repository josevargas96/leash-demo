# Leash product notes

- Owners get a push when a walk starts, when a photo is ready, and when it ends. An email receipt follows every charge.
- A walk counts as completed when it lasts at least 25 minutes and covers at least 0.5 miles. Otherwise the owner is refunded and the walker is not paid.
- Walks auto-cancel when a lightning alert is active. The owner is refunded automatically; refunds settle in 3 to 5 business days.
- Tips are on the walk summary screen. Dog profiles are under Settings > Pets.
